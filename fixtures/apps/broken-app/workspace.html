About your review workspace

This workspace provides a place where you can review positions that are available for review. To begin reviewing a position, choose the Review link next to the position you want to review. Each Review link begins the review of the position on that row. If you want to review another position instead, choose the Review link next to that other position. You can use this workspace whenever you need to review positions.
